# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short summary; the full text at that link is the one that applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act in ways that help build an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people contributing for the first time.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and ways of working, including working with AI tools.
- Give and gracefully accept helpful feedback.
- Own your mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the neighbors who use the map.

## What is not okay

- Sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a home address or email, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and the map's feedback and report forms) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, contact the project maintainer, Christine, on GitHub at @christinebuilds. You can also use the map's anonymous **Report a problem or share an idea** form. Every report will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may correct, warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior breaks this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
