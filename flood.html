A map made by neighbors to get San Francisco blocks through heavy rain.

It shows the storm drains near you and which to check first, flood reports from neighbors and from 311, places that flooded in past storms, and the city's flood zone.

You can mark a drain as cleared, thank the neighbor who cleared one, and report water on your street.
